Support

Finding a place

Type a name in the search bar on the home page and pick a suggestion. You can also tap any card or map pin to see details.

Changing who you are

Places shown depend on whether you are a student, teacher or visitor. Open the menu and choose Change user type.

Map not showing your location?

Allow location access for this website in your browser settings. Without it, the map stays centred on the main gate.

Slow to load?

If nobody has used the app for a while, the server needs up to a minute to wake up. Places you saw before are shown from your device meanwhile.
